Skeletal system

Team #4 Final Project

Team members: Mila Villarreal, Aimée García, Emiliano Gonzalez, Arturo Cabañas



The skeletal system is your body's central support framework. In adults, it consists of 206 bones.


The skeletal system provides:

  • Structure and support
  • Protection of internal organs
  • Movement with muscles
  • Production of blood cells

Without bones

  • Loss of stability
  • Respiratory failure
  • Unprotected organs
  • No movement
  • Loss of blood cell production
Diseases/Injuries

Osteoporosis

Bone disease that weakens bones by decreasing bone mass and density.

Osteomyelitis

A serious infection of the bone or bone marrow.

Bone Bruises

A painful injury that occurs when blood vessels break inside bone tissue.

Dislocations

An injury where a bone is forced out of its normal position.

Parts of a bone

Compact Bone

The dense, hard outer layer of bone.

Spongy Bone

A porous lightweight tissue found inside bones.

Red Bone Marrow

Produces blood cells inside bones.

Yellow Bone Marrow

Stores fat for energy.

Veins

Carry deoxygenated blood away.

Arteries

Supply oxygenated blood and nutrients.

Fun facts
Out of the 206 bones in the adult body, 106 are located in your hands and feet.
The hyoid bone is the only bone not attached to another bone.
You were born with around 300 bones, many of which fuse together as you grow.
The stapes in your middle ear is the smallest bone in your body.
